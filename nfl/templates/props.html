What the backtest found

Walk-forward, {{ bt.markets[0].seasons }}: every season projected by models trained only on the seasons before it. There's no free archive of past prop prices, so this scores the model against what happened, not against the market. "Line" here is the player's own trailing average, a crude opener. Books set lines with far more than that, so these rates flatter the model. They show it's competent, not that it has an edge.
