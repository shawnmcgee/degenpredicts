Ours is the published view: the market's line moved toward the model by the amount the backtest earned, which is very little. Ratings alone is the model without the market — team ratings, who is playing, rest and travel. ours % is the chance the pick wins (net of a push on a whole-number line), market % the same chance from the de-vigged price, and EV the expected return per unit at the posted price. A spread or total is flagged play only at {{ spread_min }} points of disagreement on the spread or {{ total_min }} on the total, a moneyline at {{ (ml_min * 100)|round(1) }}% EV — and only once both teams have played enough games to rate and no unresolved injury could still move the number by {{ wait_points }} points or more. Until then a pick that clears the bar is marked waiting on news. The numbers after each name are the points a game that player is worth over whoever takes his minutes.
