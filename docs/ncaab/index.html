Projected score is the model's own number before it is compared with the line. Pts off is how far the model sits from the line toward the pick: under a point is close to a coin flip. Season openers are the model's weakest games: its ratings are last season's, and the line already knows who transferred. The amber line flags what the line has to price and a rating cannot see: tournaments and neutral courts, ranked matchups, back-to-backs. Lines are ESPN's partner book.
