How to read this. The coloured bar is our probability of a home win, draw and away win — read off one scoreline distribution, so the three markets below it cannot contradict each other. A pick is flagged play only when the model disagrees with the market by more than 0.6 goals on the handicap or 0.7 on the total; those thresholds sit deliberately above every bucket the backtest could prove, so most matches show pass. Every match is still predicted, graded and CLV-tracked whether or not it is staked.
