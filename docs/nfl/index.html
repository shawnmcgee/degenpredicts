pays is what a Kalshi contract returns per unit risked after the taker fee. fair is what our model says it should return. Paying more than fair is the edge. The amber line under a matchup flags the situational facts the model weights and a glance at the line does not show: quarterback changes, rest mismatches, long trips, wind.

Best is the most valuable price for the pick across DraftKings, FanDuel, Kalshi, Polymarket, Novig, with its expected value at the model's number. Worth it at is the worst number the pick still has positive EV at, at a standard −110 price — take it there or better, and pass on anything worse. Both read off real NFL margins rather than a bell curve: about one game in seven ends on exactly 3 and one in eleven on 7, so a half-point across either (through 3) is worth several points of cover rate, and any other half-point one or two.

Teasers

Underdogs at +1.5 or +2.5, teased 6 points to +7.5 or +8.5, in games totalled 49 or less. Those 6 points cross both 3 and 7, the margins NFL games land on most, while books price every teased point alike. A two-team teaser at -120 needs each leg to win 73.9%. Sportsbooks only — exchanges don't sell teasers.
