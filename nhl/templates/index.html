The bar is our probability of each result after 60 minutes. ours is the chance the pick wins (a total's is net of a push on a whole-number line), market is the same chance from the de-vigged price, and EV is the expected return per unit at the posted price. A pick is flagged play only at EV of {{ (spread_min * 100)|round(1) }}% or more on the puck line and {{ (total_min * 100)|round(1) }}% on the total, once both teams have played enough games to rate — and only once both starting goalies are confirmed or likely. Until then a pick that clears the bar is marked waiting on goalies. Starters come from the day's news; with none yet, the model uses its own guess from recent starts and the back-to-back rule, and says how sure it is. Every goalie carries his own rating, which follows him through trades.

The last few minutes of a close game decide the puck line: a team down one or two chases, pulls its goalie, and either ties it or gives up an empty-netter. in regulation a plain Poisson model puts 30% of games on a one-goal margin, and the real figure is 18% — the rest get tied up or iced late. This model plays out the final ten minutes goal by goal.
