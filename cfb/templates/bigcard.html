How to read a card
  • Costs is the real price after the exchange's fee, written as normal odds. A Kalshi 56¢ contract costs about −137 once Kalshi's fee is added, not the −127 the 56¢ suggests. Novig charges no fee pre-game; Polymarket and Kalshi do.
  • Fair is what the bet should cost: the sportsbook's own line, plus how college games with a similar line have actually finished since 2015. It pins the main number to the market and only fills in what the line implies for every other number — so most of the time the best you can do is lose less.
  • Per $100 is the expected result of a $100 bet if that fair price is right. A standard −110 bet is about −$4.50. Green means cheaper than fair by {{ (cheap_ev * 100)|round|int }}%+ on a quote with real size.
  • Buy No: on Kalshi you can take the other side of any rung. “No” on “Alabama by 10+” is the same bet as the other team +9.5.
  • Thin means the quote has little size or a wide spread — it may not be there when you click.
